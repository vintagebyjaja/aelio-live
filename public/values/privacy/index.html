Our Values / Privacy First

Your data.
Your control.

AELIO keeps what you choose, shows you what it has, and deletes it when you ask.

Live
What it does
Opt-in memory

Nothing is remembered without your review.

Your files stay private

Uploads are processed for you, not for training.

Delete anything

Agents, memories, uploads and history.

How it works
  1. 01You choose what to share.
  2. 02You review what AELIO keeps.
  3. 03You can export or delete it any time.

Imported conversations are never used to train AELIO Native without separate, explicit consent.

Concept preview

This is how Privacy First is planned to work. It isn't operational yet, and the Brain will show it when it is.
